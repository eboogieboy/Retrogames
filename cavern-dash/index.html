Four dangerous caverns. Collect every glowing crystal, dodge the cave creatures, and reach the exit before your air runs out.

Move ← → · Jump SPACE

01 — THE COPPER PASSAGE Keyboard ready · Connect a gamepad any time PAUSE: P / START

Keyboard: ← / → or A / D to move · Space / W / ↑ to jump · P / Esc to pause · M to mute.   Controller: D-pad / stick, A to jump, Start to pause. Your progress resets when you lose a life — old-school rules!

Original game code, artwork, sound and level layouts. Inspired by 1980s single-screen platform adventures; not an official Manic Miner release. Works offline.
